User Guide

Everything you need to know to track your reading progress, save your reflections, and secure your data.

Getting Started Tracking Progress Navigation Data & Backups
1

Getting Started

The tracker runs entirely in your browser. There is no account to create and no installation required.

90-Day Plan

Focuses on the narrative flow of salvation history using the narrative books of the Bible.

365-Day Plan

A comprehensive journey designed to take you through the entire Bible over one full year.

2

Tracking Your Progress

Marking Readings as Complete

  • Individual Passages: Click on a specific scripture reference to toggle its completion status.
  • Quick Complete: Click the ✓ Complete Day button to mark all readings for that day as finished instantly.
  • Progress Bar: Track your total percentage of completion at the top of the screen.

Daily Reflections

Click the ✎ Reflection button on any day to open the notes modal. You can write prayers, study insights, or personal thoughts here. Once saved, a "Note" badge will appear on that day's card.

3

Navigation & Tools

★ Up Next Spotlight

The top card always shows you the first uncompleted day, so you can jump straight into your reading.

★ Era Filters

Filter the timeline by historical periods (e.g., "Exodus" or "The Church") using the color-coded pills.

★ Search Bar

Instantly find specific books or jump to a day number by typing in the search field.

★ Grid View

Switch to "Grid" mode for a high-level visual overview of your entire year's progress.

4

Data & Backups

⚠️ Important: Local Storage

Your progress is saved in your browser's Local Storage. This means your data is private and never leaves your device. However, if you clear your browser's cache or "site data," your progress will be deleted.

How to Export

Click the Export button in the header. This downloads a .json file to your device. We recommend doing this weekly and saving the file to a cloud drive (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.).

How to Restore

Click the Restore button and select your backup .json file. This will immediately restore all your checkboxes and reflections.

Using on Mobile?

You can "Add to Home Screen" from your browser menu to use this like a native app.
