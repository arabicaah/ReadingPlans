# 📖 The Bible Timeline Tracker

Welcome to **The Bible Timeline Tracker**, a simple, elegant tool designed to help you stay consistent with your scripture reading. Whether you are diving into the narrative flow of salvation history or committing to reading the entire Bible in a year, this tracker keeps your progress organized.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

Since this application runs entirely in your web browser, there is nothing to install. Simply open the file in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge) and start reading.

### Choosing Your Plan
At the top of the screen, you can toggle between two distinct reading journeys:
*   **90-Day Plan:** Focuses on the narrative books of the Bible, moving through the story of salvation history quickly.
*   **365-Day Plan:** A comprehensive plan to read the entire Bible over the course of a year.

---

## 🛠️ How to Use the Tracker

### 1. Tracking Your Progress
*   **Individual Passages:** Each day often has multiple readings. Click on a specific passage to check it off as you complete it.
*   **Quick Complete:** To mark an entire day as finished in one click, use the **✓ Complete Day** button in the "Up Next" spotlight or the checkbox next to the day number in the timeline.
*   **Progress Bar:** Your overall completion percentage is displayed in the header, giving you a visual sense of how far you've come.

### 2. Navigating the Timeline
*   **Up Next Spotlight:** The top card always shows you exactly where you left off, so you don't have to scroll through the list to find today's reading.
*   **Era Filters:** The Bible is divided into "Eras" (e.g., *Early World, Exodus, The Church*). Click the color-coded pills at the top to filter the timeline and focus on a specific period of history.
*   **Search:** Use the search bar to find a specific book (e.g., "Genesis" or "Luke") or jump to a specific day number.
*   **View Modes:** 
    *   **Timeline:** A detailed list grouped by historical era.
    *   **Grid:** A compact "bird's-eye view" of all days, perfect for seeing gaps in your reading.

### 3. Daily Reflections
Deepen your study by saving personal notes:
1.  Click the **✎ Reflection** button on any day.
2.  Write your prayers, insights, or questions.
3.  Click **Save Reflection**. A small "Note" badge will appear on that day to remind you that a reflection is saved.

## 💾 Managing Your Data

### Where is my progress saved?
Your progress, notes, and plan preference are saved **locally in your browser**. This means your data stays on your device and is not sent to any server. 

**⚠️ Important:** If you clear your browser's cache or "Site Data," your progress will be deleted. To prevent this, use the Backup tools.

### Backing Up and Restoring
Because the data is stored locally, we highly recommend creating regular backups:
*   **Export:** Click the **Export** button to download a small `.json` file containing all your checkboxes and notes for both plans. Store this file safely (e.g., in Google Drive, iCloud, or a folder on your PC).
*   **Restore:** If you move to a new computer or clear your browser data, click **Restore** and select your previously exported `.json` file to bring back all your progress.
